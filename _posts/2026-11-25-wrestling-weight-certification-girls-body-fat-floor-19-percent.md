If you coach girls, your lineup math changed this season. The NFHS raised the minimum body fat for female wrestlers from 12 percent to 19 percent, starting with 2026-27. The boys' floor stays at 7.

Here is what that means in the room.

## What certification is

Before a wrestler can compete, she gets certified. Two steps.

1. **Hydration test.** A urine test. Fail it and there is no assessment that day.
2. **Body fat assessment.** Her weight and body fat give her lean mass. The rules take that lean mass and work out what she would weigh at the minimum body fat.

That number is her minimum wrestling weight. It decides the lowest class she can wrestle all season. Some people call the first weigh-in the alpha weigh-in. Same thing.

## What 19 percent does

Take a girl who certifies at 130 pounds and 24 percent body fat. That is 98.8 pounds of lean mass.

- Last season, at a 12 percent floor: about 112 pounds.
- This season, at 19 percent: about 122 pounds.

Ten pounds. A weight class or two she could reach last year and cannot this year. That is the basic math; your state adds its own adjustments, so check your state association's numbers.

The NFHS said why: a higher floor gives "a safer margin for minimal wrestling weight" for girls set on a lower class.

## The descent plan still applies

Certification gives her a floor. It does not let her get there by Friday. A wrestler can lose no more than 1.5 percent of body weight a week. From 130, that is under two pounds the first week. She reaches 122 in week five, not week two.

## Where teams slip

- **Showing up dry.** Kids cut water before the test to look lighter. They fail hydration and lose the day.
- **Planning off last year's number.** A returning girl who certified at 112 may not get 112 again. Run her numbers before you promise her a class.
- **Losing track of the descent.** The plan is week by week. One kid ahead of her plan on a Tuesday is a problem you can fix. On Friday it is a forfeit.
- **Counting on the growth allowance.** Some states add two pounds after a set date. Know your state's date. Do not build a lineup on it.

## What to do this week

1. Get every certification result in one place.
2. For each girl, compare her new floor with the class she wrestled last year.
3. Tell the kids and the parents now. Not at the first dual.
4. Check every weight against the descent plan each week.
