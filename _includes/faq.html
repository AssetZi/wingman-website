FAQ

Weight cutting questions, answered.

What is the best weight cutting app for wrestlers?

Weight Wingman is built specifically for wrestlers — not dieters. It combines AI meal tracking, daily calorie and water targets that adjust to your weigh-in date, danger flags based on NCAA and NFHS weight management thresholds, and a live dashboard for coaches. It's free to start on iPhone and iPad.

How do wrestlers cut weight safely?

Safe weight cutting means losing gradually — about 1.5% of body weight per week — while staying fueled and hydrated, then using water manipulation only in the final days. Read our guides on how much weight a wrestler can safely cut per week and how to cut weight without losing strength.

Can coaches track their wrestlers' weights with the app?

Yes. Coaches get a live roster dashboard where every wrestler's weigh-ins sync in real time, with color-coded risk flags and a two-tap PDF or CSV export for compliance. Athletes join with a 6-character invite code — a whole team is set up in about 5 minutes.

No. The AI runs on-device with Apple Intelligence, so meal tracking and generation work without internet — in the wrestling room, on the bus, anywhere. The developer collects no data from the app.

Is it only for wrestling?

Weight Wingman is designed around wrestling — folkstyle, freestyle, and greco, from youth to high school to college. But any combat athlete who has to make weight, including MMA, BJJ, and boxing, can use the same weight cut planning and tracking.
